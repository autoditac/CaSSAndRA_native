Privacy Policy for CaSSAndRA Native

Effective Date: May 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

CaSSAndRA Native is a native client app designed to connect to your personal, self-hosted CaSSAndRA server via MQTT and HTTP APIs. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, location data, or usage statistics to any external servers.

2. Data Usage

All data and configurations remain entirely on your local device and the local hardware/server setup that you control. The app only processes the data required to establish a connection between your device and your personal CaSSAndRA server.

3. Third-Party Services

The application does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools that process or collect personal data.

4. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to open an issue or contact us via GitHub.
